Orbital Dynamics Employee Handbook

Vacation and leave
Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Sick leave is paid in full for up to 10 days per year without a doctor's note.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The remaining days are spent at the Thessaloniki office. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head.

Support hours
The customer support desk is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 Eastern European Time. Outside these hours, urgent incidents are routed to the on-call engineer.

On-call rotation
Engineers take part in a weekly on-call rotation. Each on-call week comes with a stipend of 150 euros. The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes.

Expenses and equipment
Expenses below 200 euros do not need prior approval, but receipts must be submitted within 30 days. Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Employees may choose between a Linux or macOS machine.
