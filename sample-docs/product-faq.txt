Orbital Dynamics Product FAQ: Atlas Platform

What is Atlas?
Atlas is our fleet-tracking platform for shipping companies. It ingests position reports from vessels and turns them into live maps, arrival estimates and compliance reports.

How often does Atlas update vessel positions?
Positions are refreshed every 30 seconds for vessels reporting over satellite and every 5 seconds for vessels within coastal radio range.

What does error E-4102 mean?
Error E-4102 means a vessel report was rejected because its timestamp is more than 10 minutes in the future. Check the onboard clock and resend the report.

Which plans are available?
Atlas is offered in three plans: Starter, Professional and Enterprise. Starter supports up to 10 vessels, Professional up to 100 vessels, and Enterprise has no vessel limit. All plans include email support; only Professional and Enterprise include a dedicated account manager.

What is the API rate limit?
The public API allows 600 requests per minute per API key. Requests above the limit receive HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header.

Can I export data?
Yes. Reports can be exported as CSV or PDF. Bulk exports of more than 1 million rows must be requested through support and are delivered within 2 business days.

Which regions host Atlas?
Atlas runs in Frankfurt and Dublin. Customers can choose the region at signup; data does not leave the selected region.
