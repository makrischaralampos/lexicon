# Orbital Dynamics Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless the travel desk approved them in writing.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and requires approval from a director. Flight changes caused by the traveller are paid by the traveller.

## Hotels and meals

The hotel limit is 140 euros per night in Europe and 220 euros per night in the rest of the world. A daily meal allowance of 45 euros applies on every day of a business trip; alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or trains whenever possible. A rental car is allowed only when no public transport is available. Private cars used for business are reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre.

## Approval and reimbursement

Trips with a total estimated cost above 1500 euros require written approval from a director before booking. Expense reports for a trip must be filed within 14 days of returning, together with all receipts. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Client entertainment

Client dinners are limited to 80 euros per person and must be pre-approved by the account manager. Gifts to clients may not exceed 50 euros in value.
