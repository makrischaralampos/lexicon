# Orbital Dynamics IT Onboarding Guide

## First day

Collect your laptop from the IT desk on your first day. Install the company password manager, enroll your hardware security key and connect to the VPN before requesting any other access. VPN sessions expire after 12 hours and must be renewed.

## Access requests

All access requests are filed as tickets with the prefix IT- in the service portal. Standard requests are completed within 2 business days. Requests for production access also need approval from the team lead.

## Passwords and keys

Service account passwords are rotated every 90 days by the platform team. The guest Wi-Fi key is changed monthly and is 10 characters long. Hardware security keys that are lost must be reported to the IT desk the same day.

## Hardware

Standard laptops come with 16 GB of RAM; developers receive machines with 32 GB of RAM. Peripherals such as monitors and keyboards can be ordered once through the equipment catalog. Broken devices are replaced within 3 business days.

## IT helpdesk

The IT helpdesk is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 Eastern European Time. Urgent outages outside these hours are reported through the on-call chat channel.

## Software

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed. Other tools need a request ticket and a short security review, which takes up to 5 business days.
